The catalog describes dependency classes, not ownership. Application-specific documentation and research artifacts remain in their canonical repositories.

Execution-mode and CI-workflow references above are drawn from each repository's public workflow files, not from the workload's internal implementation; a repository having CI does not imply its GPU/FPGA/simulation workload itself runs inside that CI. Provenance hooks and CI references marked "Planned" or "once #N merges" name an artifact that does not exist on `main` yet (#3, #7); update the row once that artifact merges and is actually consumed by the workload.
